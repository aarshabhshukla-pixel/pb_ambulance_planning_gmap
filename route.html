Search a place on the map, or press Add stops and click the map. Times recalculate as you change speed, halt, order or start time.

Map & scenario

Coverage shape is saved with each route. Map settings are remembered in this browser. Share links carry the open route (stops and settings) and open for any signed-in admin. PDF report opens the print dialog; choose “Save as PDF”. Save scenario keeps everything (ambulances, staff, hospitals & bases, routes, deployments and saved Radius layouts) in one file. If the map shows a warning instead of streets, pick another base map.

Roster

Click an empty cell to deploy that ambulance on that day. Its default pilot and EMT (set in Ambulances & staff) and the route it used last are filled in for you. Red outline = clash: the same ambulance or person in two deployments at overlapping times.

Bulk add or update (fastest way)

Copy rows from Excel (including the header row) and paste below, or upload a CSV / Excel file. Existing records are matched by registration number (ambulances) or employee code, else phone, else name (staff), so nothing is duplicated. Default pilot and EMT can be given by name, phone or employee code.

Ambulances

Default crew is used automatically when you deploy this ambulance. Changing a dropdown saves at once. These ambulances also appear in the Radius coverage module (Fleet → Sync with ambulance master).

Hospitals & bases

Hospitals, ambulance bases and standby points show on every map. Pick one as an ambulance’s base below, or add it to a route as a stop (click it on the map). These hospitals also sync into the Radius coverage module.

Population grid (optional)

Load a CSV of population points (columns lat, lng, pop; e.g. ward or census-village centres, or a WorldPop grid exported to CSV). Then every stop can take its population from the grid within its radius, and the route summary shows unique population with overlaps counted once. Without a grid, enter population by hand.
